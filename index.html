About

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Bioengineering at Imperial College London, where my research focuses on robust artificial intelligence methods for medical imaging.

My current work primarily investigates low-dose PET imaging, including image restoration, diffusion-based reconstruction, lesion-preserving enhancement, uncertainty-aware modelling, and clinically meaningful quantitative evaluation.

More broadly, I am interested in generative models, multimodal medical imaging, medical image synthesis, reconstruction, segmentation, and the development of AI systems that remain reliable under real-world clinical distribution shifts.

Research

Low-Dose PET Imaging

Developing deep-learning methods for ultra-low-dose PET restoration and reconstruction while preserving quantitative radiotracer uptake and clinically relevant lesion information.

Generative Medical Imaging

Investigating diffusion models and other generative approaches for image reconstruction, denoising, enhancement, and cross-modality synthesis.

Robust & Clinical AI

Studying reliability, uncertainty, lesion-level evaluation, segmentation, and domain robustness for medical imaging AI systems intended for clinical use.
